Most of what I learn at work evaporates within a month. The fix that took a day to find, the reason a design was rejected, the one flag that makes a tool behave: gone, unless I write it down.

So this is where I'll write it down. Some posts will be essays like this one, some will be short journal entries, and every so often there will be a longer technical write-up with code and numbers.

## What to expect

Posts are filed under one of three kinds, so you can follow just the ones you care about:

- **Blog** for essays and opinions.
- **Journal** for short, dated notes on what I worked on and learned.
- **Technical** for deep dives with code, measurements, and conclusions.

Each kind has its own RSS feed if you prefer to subscribe.
